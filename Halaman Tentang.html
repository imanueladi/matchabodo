What is Matchabodo?

Matchabodo kicked off in 2025 when a group of matcha-obsessed friends got tired of seeing great matcha locked behind crazy price tags. We started with one simple idea: everyone deserves a fresh, delicious, and authentic matcha fix without breaking the bank. What began as late-night tasting sessions and endless recipe tweaks quickly turned into a daily mission to bring real, no-nonsense matcha to the neighborhood.

It’s All About the Leaf (and the Math) We don’t cut corners when it comes to what goes into your cup. We source carefully selected matcha powder with that rich, vibrant green color and smooth umami kick—none of that bitter, chalky stuff. To make sure your favorite drink never misses, we keep our measurements spot-on down to the gram. Whether you’re ordering your first cup or your fiftieth, you get the exact same creamy, balanced flavor every single time.

Everything is made by hand every day, because great matcha always starts from scratch..

Thanks for dropping by Matchabodo!.
